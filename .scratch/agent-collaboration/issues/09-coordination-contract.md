## Question

How will an existing Coordinator discover, launch, address, prompt, wait for,
interrupt, and obtain results from Workers through the plugin? Choose a CLI,
MCP, local API, or justified combination after considering the research. Define
session/task identity, result delivery and late events, caller authority, and
how the same session remains available in its visible Pane. Decide per-adapter
control strategies without assuming that a headless API controls a native TUI.
