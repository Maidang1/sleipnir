## Question

Does the initial experience manage only agents it launches, or also adopt
already-running agents? Define its boundary across Tabs, Windows, projects, and
machines, intended supported operating systems, and expected behavior when a
pane, coordinator, plugin, or terminal closes. Clarify whether any restart
recovery is required without silently restoring terminal scrollback or changing
the existing session-persistence boundary.
