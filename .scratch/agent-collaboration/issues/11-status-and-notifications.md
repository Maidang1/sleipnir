## Question

Using a throwaway interaction prototype, which compact overview, pane indicators,
and notifications make a Coordinator and several visible Workers understandable
without obscuring their terminal interfaces? Cover active work, human input or
approval required, newly finished work, stale/unknown state, and direct navigation
or takeover. Decide notification triggers and acknowledgement behavior with the
human rather than preselecting a UI or treating progress as a percentage.
